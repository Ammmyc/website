Dr. Amy H.N. Cheung
Assistant Professor
The University of Hong Kong
PhD Clinical Psychology Chartered Psychologist (BPS) Principal Investigator

A psychometric specialist known for developing the Multidimensional Depression Assessment Scale (MDAS), Dr. Cheung leads innovative research on depression, self-compassion, and mental wellbeing across adolescents, high-ability youth, and older adults — with a focus on cultural and gender differences in interpersonal symptoms.

Focus Areas

Research Interests

Depression & Self-Compassion
Interpersonal symptoms of depression, self-compassion frameworks, and cultural and gender differences across populations.
Adolescents & High-Ability Youth
Mental wellbeing in high-achieving youth, positive thinking programmes, and school-based interventions for Hong Kong adolescents.
Ageing & End-of-Life Care
Compassion-based interventions for the elderly, end-of-life psychological care, and mental health support for older adults.
AI & Mental Health Education
AI-enhanced mental health education platforms and innovative interventions for mental health literacy.
Psychometrics & Scale Development
Developer of the MDAS (Multidimensional Depression Assessment Scale), validated across cultures and languages worldwide.
Cross-Cultural Psychology
Cultural and gender differences in psychological symptoms, assessment, and clinical interventions across East Asian and Western contexts.
